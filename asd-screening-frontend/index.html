ASD Screening Assistant Prototype · not a diagnosis

A quick check on how your child communicates and plays

Answer 10 short questions about everyday behaviour. It takes about 5 minutes. This prototype provides screening support and does not diagnose autism.

  • The result is a screening signal, not a diagnosis.
